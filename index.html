A CENTURY OF EDUCATIONAL EXCELLENCE

Welcome to UPGS Punukkannoor

Established in 1926, UPGS Punukkannoor has proudly served generations of learners for a century, standing as a beacon of academic excellence, strong values, and community spirit. We blend a rich educational heritage with modern learning approaches to nurture curiosity, creativity, discipline, and confidence in every child. As we celebrate 100 years of learning and growth, we remain committed to inspiring young minds and shaping a brighter future for generations to come.

Play & Learn Wing
EARLY CHILDHOOD EDUCATION & CARE

Joyful Learning at UPGS Kindergarten

At UPGS Kindergarten, we provide a warm, nurturing, and joyful first step into education. Our activity-based curriculum blends playful exploration with foundational language, numeracy, and creative expression. With child-friendly digital smart tools, sensory play areas, and dedicated caring educators, we inspire wonder and build confidence in our youngest learners, laying a strong foundation for lifelong learning.
