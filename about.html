About UPGS Punukkonnoor

U.P.G. School, Punukkonnoor (School Code: 41660) is a well-established aided educational institution located in the scenic surroundings of Kollam, Kerala, under the Kundara Sub-District. Established in 1926, the school has proudly served generations of students and families, growing from humble beginnings into a trusted centre of learning, discipline, values, and community development.

For nearly a century, UPGS Punukkonnoor has remained committed to providing children with a nurturing and inspiring environment where education extends beyond textbooks and classrooms. Our journey is a reflection of the dedication of generations of teachers, managers, parents, and well-wishers who have contributed to the growth and development of the institution.

HERITAGE MILESTONES

Our History & Heritage

Historical Background

The story of UPGS Punukkonnoor began in 1926, when the institution started its journey with classes 1 and 2. As the educational needs of the community grew, the school continued to expand and develop over the years.

During 1958–59, the school received official recognition up to Class 5, marking an important milestone in its educational journey. Subsequently, during 1966–67, recognition was extended to Classes 6 and 7, and the institution was officially upgraded as an Upper Primary School. From these modest beginnings, the school has continued to grow while preserving the educational values and traditions that have shaped its identity.

A Legacy Built by Generations

The history of UPGS Punukkonnoor is inseparable from the teachers and educational leaders who dedicated their lives to the institution. Around 50 teachers who served the school over the years and went on to retire as Headmasters are now living in retirement.

Their contributions remain an important part of our school's heritage. We gratefully remember the former teachers, Headmasters, managers, and other members of the school community who laid the foundations upon which the institution continues to grow today.
